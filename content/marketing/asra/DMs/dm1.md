Hi [Name] — I'm Ilakk, founder of **Nature Foundation Models**.

I'm building something I care deeply about: **scientific intelligence that reasons from interaction, not just prediction.**

Most AI in science fits patterns on static data. Real discovery needs systems that **observe, infer hidden structure, run interventions, and revise hypotheses** — especially when objectives and action semantics are unknown.

At the center is **ASRA** (Adaptive Scientific Reasoning Architecture): a modular cognitive stack for adaptive reasoning in unfamiliar environments — world models, causal inference, exploration, and mechanism discovery. We're stress-testing it on ARC-AGI and extending it into **Decision Biology**, where perturbations are actions and cellular pathways are world models.

This isn't an LLM wrapper. It's infrastructure for **how science actually works** — and a path toward foundation models for natural systems.

I'm building full-time, publishing openly, and shipping incrementally (research library + competition agents + SciLayer preprints). Early, but the architecture is real and progressing phase by phase.

I'd value your perspective — whether that's advice, an intro, or support as we scale. Happy to share a 10-min overview or send the short white paper.
