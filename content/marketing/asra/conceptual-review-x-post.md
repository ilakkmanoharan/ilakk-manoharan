New conceptual review: how AI systems can move from prediction to **scientific reasoning under uncertainty**.

It synthesizes world models, causal inference, active inference, information theory, and perturbation biology into one architecture story—not a benchmark paper, but a map of what adaptive scientific intelligence actually requires.

Concepts covered:
• Learned world models (Dreamer, MuZero)
• Active inference & the free-energy principle
• Causal inference & intervention semantics (Pearl)
• Information-theoretic experiment selection
• Action semantics & transition modeling
• Evidence memory & model revision
• Perturbation biology (LINCS, scPerturb, Human Cell Atlas)
• Skill-acquisition efficiency (Chollet / ARC-style intelligence)
• Open gaps: causal representation learning, calibrated uncertainty, scientific benchmarks
