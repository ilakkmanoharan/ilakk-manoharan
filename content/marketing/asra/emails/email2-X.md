Building Nature Foundation Models — adaptive scientific intelligence for natural systems.

Applied to YC S26. Building full-time either way.

Most AI in science optimizes pattern prediction. Real discovery needs intervention reasoning, causal structure, and learning under uncertainty.

At the center: ASRA (Adaptive Scientific Reasoning Architecture) — world models, causal inference, intervention learning, adaptive experimentation.

First domain: Decision Biology — perturbations as interventions, signaling as world models, cellular adaptation as causal reasoning.

Not another LLM wrapper. ASRA learns what actions *mean* from interaction — then proposes, tests, and revises hypotheses.

Progressing ASRA v0.1 → progressively more capable adaptive reasoning systems. Solo founder. Year zero.
