**What I'm building**

I'm building **Nature Foundation Models** — adaptive scientific intelligence for natural systems. Most AI in science today is optimized for **pattern prediction**. Actual scientific discovery requires something harder: choosing interventions, learning causal structure, updating beliefs from experiments, and discovering hidden mechanisms under uncertainty.

At the center is **ASRA** (Adaptive Scientific Reasoning Architecture): a reasoning engine for world-model construction, causal inference, intervention learning, and adaptive experimentation.

Our first domain is **Decision Biology** — treating cells as adaptive decision systems. We model:

- perturbations as **interventions**
- signaling pathways as **world models**
- cellular adaptation as **causal reasoning**

This is not another LLM wrapper or prediction pipeline. ASRA learns **what actions mean** from interaction itself — then uses that understanding to propose, test, and revise scientific hypotheses.

Long term, Nature Foundation Models becomes a **multi-domain scientific intelligence platform** spanning biology, chemistry, physics, and environmental systems.

**Why now**

1. Foundation models proved large-scale representation learning works.
2. Biology is becoming an intervention-rich state space — single-cell atlases, perturbation compendia, and multimodal datasets make closed-loop reasoning possible for the first time.
3. Scientific AI is shifting from prediction toward reasoning and experimentation — but most systems still assume predefined action spaces and known environment semantics.

**Where I am today**

I've spent nearly 20 years building production systems across AI, distributed infrastructure, cloud, and scientific computing. I recently started working full-time on ASRA, Decision Biology, and the research foundation behind Nature Foundation Models:

- Published conceptual architecture and research direction (including a review on adaptive scientific reasoning under uncertainty)
- Early prototypes: transition logging, world-model experiments, adaptive reasoning loops

I'm building the **cognitive substrate** for scientific intelligence — progressing ASRA from v0.1 toward progressively more capable adaptive reasoning systems, with each iteration compounding into stronger scientific discovery tools.

**Business model**

The core business is **foundational STEM research** — world models, causal inference, intervention semantics, and uncertainty-aware experimentation across natural systems. Downstream, that research enables platform products: scientific reasoning infrastructure, adaptive research platforms, domain-specific foundation models, and enterprise scientific AI systems.

Decision Biology is the wedge. Nature Foundation Models is the platform.
