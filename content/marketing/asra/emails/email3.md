Hi Jason — congrats on wrapping LA36. Year zero / year one support is the hardest work in this business, and I appreciate that Launch makes space for founders still early in that journey.

I applied to YC S26 for **Nature Foundation Models** (currently under Bubbles & Cafe Inc. — renaming soon) and haven't heard back yet. YC has been a long-standing dream for me — not as a credential, but as the environment this company needs: ambitious peers, relentless focus on what matters, and a community that gets deep, long-horizon technical work. I'm building either way, but I'd also sincerely welcome the chance to talk with you about Launch.

**What I'm building**

**Nature Foundation Models** — adaptive scientific intelligence for natural systems. Most AI in science optimizes pattern prediction. Real discovery needs choosing interventions, learning causal structure, updating beliefs from experiments, and finding hidden mechanisms under uncertainty.

At the center is **ASRA** (Adaptive Scientific Reasoning Architecture): world-model construction, causal inference, intervention learning, adaptive experimentation.

First domain: **Decision Biology** — cells as adaptive decision systems. Perturbations as interventions, signaling pathways as world models, cellular adaptation as causal reasoning. Not another LLM wrapper — ASRA learns what actions *mean* from interaction, then proposes, tests, and revises hypotheses.

Long term: multi-domain scientific intelligence across biology, chemistry, physics, and environmental systems. Decision Biology is the wedge; Nature Foundation Models is the platform.

**Why now:** foundation models proved representation learning at scale; biology is becoming intervention-rich (single-cell atlases, perturbation compendia); scientific AI is shifting from prediction toward reasoning — but most systems still assume predefined action spaces.

**Where I am:** ~20 years in production AI, distributed infra, cloud, and scientific computing. Recently full-time on ASRA and the research foundation — published architecture and direction, early prototypes (transition logging, world-model experiments, adaptive reasoning loops), public repo and site live. Solo founder, year zero.

Happy to forward my YC application, share a deck, or jump on a call.
