**ASRA-LoRA** teaches foundation models how to *reason* — not just predict answers. Instead of fine-tuning on input → output pairs alone, the project generates synthetic traces that capture observation, hypothesis, exploration, failure, revision, and solution.

**Shipped:** seven JSONL datasets (**242,247 rows**), regeneration scripts, interactive portfolio visualizations, and a Qwen2.5-1.5B LoRA training pipeline. Sources include ASRA transition logs, archived Kaggle submission post-mortems, and Original ARC task pairs.
