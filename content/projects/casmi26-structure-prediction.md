**Enveda CASMI 2026 — Molecular Structure Identification** is a neuro-symbolic research system for identifying molecular structures from mass spectra in the [Enveda CASMI 2026](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra) Kaggle competition. It combines deep-learning spectral models with symbolic chemical constraints, candidate ranking, and an ASRA-aligned experimental loop for hypothesis generation, error analysis, and iterative submissions.
