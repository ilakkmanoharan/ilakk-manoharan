Publish Desk is a web platform for publishers and teams who want **magazine-style** reading and organization—not only a flat blog.

Authors write in **Markdown**, often from **Git**, and sync that content into the app; it is stored as structured articles linked to **magazines**, with categories and tags.

A **dashboard** (after sign-in) is where publishers manage content, magazines, scheduling, GitHub as a source, and account settings.

A **public site** lets readers browse magazines and articles per publisher, with layouts from standard prose to magazine-style columns and optional comic-style panels.
**Premium** and **private-link** options support teasers, gates, and hiding pieces from public listings when desired.

The stack is **Next.js**, **React**, and **TypeScript** on the front; **Firebase Authentication** and **Firestore** provide accounts and multi-tenant data.

Server **API routes** with **Firebase Admin** run trusted work such as **GitHub sync**—parsing Markdown, updating the database, and stabilizing images for the reader.
The UI uses **Tailwind CSS** and Markdown rendering so articles look polished on both dashboard previews and the live site.

**Git-friendly authoring**, **editorial presentation**, and **cloud-backed publishing**—in one product.
