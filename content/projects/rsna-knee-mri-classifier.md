**RSNA Knee MRI Classifier** is a deep-learning system for detecting knee abnormalities from MRI exams in the [RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection) Kaggle competition. It combines multi-view medical-image classification with ASRA-inspired symbolic representations to improve diagnostic reasoning and interpretability.
