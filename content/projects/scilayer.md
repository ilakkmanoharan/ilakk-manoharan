**SciLayer** is an open archive for scientific papers across every field—preprints, research articles, reviews, datasets, and methods papers.

Researchers **sign in with ORCID**, submit manuscripts from **GitHub**, and move work through **validation**, **classification**, **peer review**, and **public publication**.

Built as a **Next.js** journal platform with article pages, author profiles, editor and reviewer dashboards, and discovery by **search**, **tags**, and **subject taxonomy**.

The stack is **Next.js 16**, **React**, and **TypeScript** with **Tailwind CSS**; **Prisma** and **PostgreSQL** back the data model; **ORCID OAuth** handles researcher identity; **remark** and **Zod** support manuscript content and validation on the submission pipeline.
