**Smart Restaurant powered by Cloud-Native IoT AIOS** is described in U.S. patent application publication **US 2024/0273653 A1** (Google Patents: [US20240273653A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240273653A1/en)).

The filing covers an architecture that combines **IoT** at the venue with a **cloud-native** control and data plane and an **AI-oriented operating model** suited to high-churn restaurant workflows—sensors, connectivity, and software services aligned for monitoring, automation, and intelligence-driven operations.
