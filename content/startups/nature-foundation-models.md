**Nature Foundation Models** is Ilak's long-term bet on AI infrastructure for scientific intelligence—learn the structure of reality through interaction.

The program page at **[/nfm](/nfm)** covers the full stack: **NFM** → **NFM-Worlds** → **NFM-Robotics** → **Atlas** → **Atlas-GS** (v1 implemented: 0.0102 m localization RMSE on TUM RGB-D), plus **ASRA** (nine-phase adaptive scientific reasoning) and links to all **SciLayer** preprints.

Phase 1 wedges into **Decision Biology**: treating cells as adaptive information-processing systems where perturbations are interventions and pathways are latent world models.
