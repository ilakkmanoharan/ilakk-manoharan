**Nature Foundation Models** is Ilak's long-term bet on AI infrastructure for scientific intelligence across biology, chemistry, physics, and environmental systems—not benchmark-bound prediction, but models that learn natural-system dynamics, simulate under uncertainty, and support discovery workflows.

Phase 1 wedges into **Decision Biology**: treating cells as adaptive information-processing systems where perturbations are interventions and pathways are latent world models. This connects directly to **ASRA** (Adaptive Scientific Reasoning Architecture) and the exceptional-ability research narrative on ilakk-manoharan.vercel.app.
