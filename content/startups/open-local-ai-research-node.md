**Open Local AI Research Node for Autonomous Neuro-Symbolic Science** makes locally owned compute practical for sustained AI-for-science and AI-safety research.

The node is the infrastructure. ASRA is the flagship reasoning system: Tabby inquires, Grubby handles evidence, and Sam decides. Together they observe, hypothesize, intervene, evaluate, and update an explicit world model.
