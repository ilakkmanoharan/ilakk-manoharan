**TOMO** is the bounded tomato-monitoring scenario in Ilakkuvaselvi Manoharan's Perspective, **The World as a Graph Database for Distributed Robotic Intelligence and Autonomous Optimization** (November 1, 2026).

[Project page](/projects/tomo) · [Open-access paper](https://zenodo.org/records/23873426) · [Concept and images](https://x.com/ilakkManoharan/status/2117256931920257499) · [Original vision](https://x.com/ilakkManoharan/status/2116951780634624098)

The proposal is hypothetical. No operating prototype, experimental results, or demonstrated energy savings are claimed.
