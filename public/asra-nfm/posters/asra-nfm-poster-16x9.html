Central question. Can an agent learn unfamiliar environments efficiently by treating actions as scientific interventions, maintaining competing explanations, and explicitly modeling the mechanisms that produce observed changes?

Motivation

  • Pattern recognition may predict without discovering mechanisms.
  • Purely symbolic systems are brittle under uncertain perception.
  • Passive learning is inefficient in novel interactive settings.
  • Agents must learn action meaning, goals, mechanisms, and plan under incomplete knowledge.
