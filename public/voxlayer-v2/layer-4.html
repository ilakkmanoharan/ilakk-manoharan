Layer 4

Dialogue — the script

"What caller and agent say"

Layer 4 produces spoken English — caller lines and receptionist lines. It does not choose the next state (Layer 2 planned that). It does not build messages[] (Layer 3). Think scriptwriter vs director vs editor.

Shipped — mode llm (prefix + target) via Qwen; templates for offline
